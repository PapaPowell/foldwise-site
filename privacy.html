FoldWise Privacy Policy

What stays on your phone

Hinge readings, fold counts, checkups, crease ratings and daily history stay on your device. FoldWise has no accounts, ads or tracking.

Owner comparison, only if you turn it on

FoldWise sends your phone's age group, your averages, latest checkup numbers and hinge sensor timing to a public database in Apple's CloudKit, stored under a random ID. Other FoldWise users can read these rows to work out comparisons. It doesn't send your name, location or daily activity. The purpose is owner comparison and improving accuracy.

Cold folds, only if you turn it on

Your approximate location is sent to Apple's WeatherKit to get the temperature. FoldWise doesn't store or share your location.

Help and suggestions

If you email us from the app, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply and improve FoldWise. Problem reports include your app and iOS versions, shown in the email before you send it.

Purchases

Apple handles all payments. FoldWise never sees your payment details.

Keeping and deleting data

Your shared row stays until you turn sharing off, which deletes it. Data on your phone stays until you use Reset All Data in Settings or delete the app.

Changing your mind

You can turn off sharing or cold folds at any time, and turn off location access in iOS Settings.
