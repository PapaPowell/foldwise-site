Why doesn't my fold count match what I actually did?

iOS only shares hinge data while FoldWise is open. To count closes the rest of the time, set up the automation under Settings, Count every fold.

Can FoldWise tell me if my hinge is failing?

No. It records how your hinge behaves and what you see, so you can notice changes. Only Apple can assess your phone. If you see lines, dots or dead areas on the screen, contact Apple Support.

Does it work on a regular iPhone?

Only as a demo. Real tracking needs a foldable iPhone.

How does the free trial work?

You can try every Pro feature free for 7 days, once. Nothing renews and you won't be charged. When it ends, Pro features lock unless you unlock Pro with a one-time payment. Your data stays.

Why don't I see a projection yet?

It appears after 3 days and 5 folds, once FoldWise knows your pace.

Is 200,000 folds how long my phone will last?

No. Apple hasn't published a fold rating, so 200,000 is a yardstick, not a prediction.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap Restore Purchase on any Pro feature.
